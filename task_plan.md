## Phase 3: Architect & Style (Core Product Pillars)

### Pillar 1: Behavioral Intelligence & Daily Support
- [ ] **Dashboard (`/dashboard`):** High-level overview, today's summary, next-day plan snippet, quick actions.
- [ ] **Daily Check-In (`/check-in`):** Polished logging interface for sleep, meals, mood, meltdowns, sensory, routines.
- [ ] **Behavioral Dashboard (`/insights`):** Beautiful, calm charts visualizing sleep, mood, and sensory patterns over time.
- [ ] **Daily Planner (`/planner`):** Detailed view of the next-day routine, food plan, and sensory support generated by DAYA.
- [ ] **Chatbot Integration (`/chat`):** Ensure the existing chat is accessible and prominent.

### Pillar 2: Services & Continuity (Booking Ecosystem)
- [ ] **Schools Directory (`/services/schools`):** Autism-friendly school cards, admission request flow, success state.
- [ ] **Therapist Directory (`/services/therapy`):** Therapist profiles, filtering by specialty, slot booking flow, success state.
- [ ] **Shadow Teachers (`/services/shadow-teachers`):** Profiles, availability, request callback flow.

### Pillar 3: Parenting & Community
- [ ] **Communities Page (`/community`):** Safe support spaces, groups.
- [ ] **Workshops (`/community/workshops`):** Webinar bookings, coaching sessions, confirmation state.

### Pillar 4: Continuity of Care (Reports)
- [ ] **Doctor Reports (`/reports`):** Clean, professional summaries of 30-day patterns, triggers, and stability indexes. Exportable layout.

### Ancillary Pages
- [ ] **Landing Page (`/`):** Update to market the complete ecosystem.
- [ ] **Auth Pages (`/login`, `/signup`):** Calm, premium onboarding.
- [ ] **Settings/Profile (`/settings`):** Child profile configuration, parent preferences.

## Phase 4: Trigger (Final Polish)
